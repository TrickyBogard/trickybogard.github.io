Send WhatsApp Messages Without Saving Contacts — A Practical Guide for Couriers, Small Businesses & Privacy-Focused Users

Don't want to save a temporary number to your contacts? Quick Message lets you start direct chats on WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Viber and SMS without saving the number. Includes on-device OCR, dynamic templates, secure vault and scheduled reminders.

Introduction

Need to message a friend, courier, or one-off customer but don't want to save their number to your contacts? Your address book fills up with temporary numbers you'll never call again. Quick Message is an Android utility that lets you start direct messaging-app conversations without saving contacts — keeping your address book clean.

This guide explains the app's real features and how to use them, step by step. All claims are verified against the app's source code and Google Play store listing. No misleading or non-existent feature claims are made.

Real Features

1. Fast Send — No Contact Saving Needed

Select a country code, enter a phone number, and launch WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Viber or SMS directly. Clipboard handoff is supported for WeChat. The number is not added to your contacts; your address book stays free of couriers, temporary clients, and one-off inquiries.

Source: PRODUCT.md — Multi-Platform Direct Messaging; store_listing_copy_tr_en.md.

2. Smart OCR — Camera Number & Text Scanner

Scan phone numbers from business cards, printed documents, or signs using your camera. Powered by Google ML Kit on-device text recognition: images are processed entirely on your phone and never sent to cloud servers. Transfer recognized numbers to the phone field, or scanned text to the compose canvas, in a single tap.

Source: PRODUCT.md — On-Device ML Kit OCR; store_listing_copy_tr_en.md.

3. Dynamic Templates with Smart Variables

Save frequently sent customer messages, appointment notes, and order notifications as templates. Use dynamic placeholder chips like [Customer Name], [Order No], [Delivery Time], and [Location] to customize messages in seconds through a simple form. Pin, edit, and organize your template library.

Source: FEATURE_MATRIX_B.md — Template Pack Service; store_listing_copy_tr_en.md.

4. Secure Vault

Keep confidential customer information, private notes, or credentials safe. Encrypted with AES-256-GCM using keys stored in the Android Keystore. Lock and unlock with your personal 4-digit PIN or biometric fingerprint. Your encrypted records stay exclusively on your phone; nothing is uploaded to servers.

Source: PRODUCT.md — Hardware Keystore Secure Vault; store_listing_copy_tr_en.md.

5. Scheduled Message Reminders

Plan ahead by setting time-based reminders for important messages. Receive a local notification at the scheduled time; tap the notification to review and send the message instantly via your preferred messaging app. Important note: the app never automatically sends messages in the background. It functions as a reminder — you remain in complete control of sending.

Source: PRODUCT.md — Scheduled Reminder Notifications; store_listing_copy_tr_en.md.

6. Other Features

  • Saved Locations & Map Links: Save your favorite office, warehouse, or pickup locations with custom titles and map links. Quickly append saved location links to any outgoing message. (The app does not track background GPS; location sharing relies solely on your manually saved entries).
  • Digital Card (vCard) & QR Code: Create your digital contact card and generate a personal QR code. Let clients scan your QR code to instantly import your contact card into their address book.
  • Encrypted Backup & Export: Back up your library with password-protected AES-256 (.enc) encryption, or export as encrypted (.qmenc) or JSON files.
  • Privacy & Data Safety: Zero server footprint — no accounts, no message uploads. Your local app data is excluded from automated cloud backups (allowBackup=false).

Usage Scenarios

Courier / Delivery Driver

While on the road, sends a template with order number, delivery time and customer location via WhatsApp in one tap; the customer's number is not added to contacts.

Small Business Owner

Fills in the Order Confirmation template with customer name and order number in the morning and sends it via WhatsApp; keeps the address book clean.

General Android User

Scans a business card with the camera, the number lands in the composer, writes the message and sends via WhatsApp; the number is never written to contacts and notes stay encrypted in the vault.
