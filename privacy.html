Privacy Policy for Quick Message

Quick Message is provided by Kenan Ergin (TrickyBogard) as a Free/Freemium app.

1. Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal data, phone numbers, or contact lists. All processes happen locally on your device.

2. Location Information

The app allows you to share your location. This data is only used to generate a Google Maps link for your message and is not stored or tracked by us.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app uses official APIs (like WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber, Signal, WeChat, SMS) to initiate chats. We are not responsible for the data practices of these platforms. Additionally, the app uses third-party services like Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may collect anonymous device identifiers and advertising data.
